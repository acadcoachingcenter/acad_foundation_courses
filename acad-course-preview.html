1.1 What is Electric Charge? NCERT §1.1–1.2
Electric charge: intrinsic property causing electromagnetic force experience.
Two types: Positive (+) and Negative (−). Like repel; unlike attract.
SI Unit: Coulomb (C). One coulomb = charge of ~6.24 × 10¹⁸ electrons.
e⁻: q = −1.6×10⁻¹⁹ C; Proton: q = +1.6×10⁻¹⁹ C.
Charging = transfer of electrons. Methods: Friction, Conduction, Induction.
⚡ Key Facts
e = 1.6×10⁻¹⁹ C
Unit: Coulomb
Scalar quantity
q = ne
Conserved
Quantised

3.1 Coulomb's Law — Formula NCERT §1.5
Scalar Form
F = kq₁q₂/r²

2.1 Electric Potential — Definition NCERT §2.1–2.2
V = W/q₀ — work done per unit +ve charge from ∞ to the point.
SI Unit: Volt (V) = J/C. Scalar quantity — no direction.
Point charge: V = kq/r. V ∝ 1/r (E ∝ 1/r²).
Superposition: V = V₁ + V₂ + ... + Vₙ (scalar sum — simpler!).
Path-independent: V is a state function (conservative field).
🔋 Key Facts
V = kq/r
k = 9×10⁹
Unit: Volt
Scalar qty
V=0 at ∞
+q → V>0
